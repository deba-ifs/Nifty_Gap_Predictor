Overnight Holding Window Architecture (12:00 PM IST → 09:20 AM IST)

Scope of exposure, event filtration logic, and overnight gap risk management.

21h 20m Holding Duration
1. Entry & Signal Setup (12:00 PM IST)

Positions are entered at 12:00 PM IST based on the 11:30 AM IST quant model forecast. The model evaluates live domestic market momentum, options sentiment (PCR), and early offshore cues.

2. Overnight Gap Exposure

The holding period spans 21 hours and 20 minutes overnight. During this closed-market block, trades are exposed exclusively to overnight global news, US equity returns, and geopolitical shocks.

3. Exit & Verification (09:20 AM IST)

Trades are strictly closed at 09:20 AM IST at market open. All events scheduled after 09:20 AM IST (e.g. 10:00 AM policy speeches) carry zero risk and are ignored by the model.

Primary Overnight Threat Vectors Evaluated
  • US Inflation & FOMC Rate Decision Releases (18:30 – 23:30 IST): Scheduled overnight US macro releases directly shift US futures and S&P 500 closing returns.
  • Overnight Brent Crude Oil Spikes (`BZ=F` > +2.0%): NYMEX/ICE energy market moves occurring while Indian markets are closed impact morning opening sentiment.
  • Offshore GIFT Nifty Arbitrage (06:30 – 09:15 AM IST): Pre-market GIFT Nifty prices incorporate global overnight news prior to the local opening bell.

2. Prediction Formulas & Event Multiplier

Mathematical transparency detailing how predicted open prices and event-adjusted 1-sigma bounds are calculated.

Step A: Stationary Target Transformation

To prevent machine learning trees from stalling at historical ceilings, the model forecasts the stationary gap percentage ($\hat{g}$):

$\hat{g} = \widehat{\Delta \text{Gap}\%}_{t+1} = f\Big(\text{CLV}_t, \text{VIX}_t, \text{GIFT}_t, \text{PCR}_t, \text{RSI}_t, \text{Crude}_t, \text{USDINR}_t\Big)$
Step B: Reconstructing Predicted Opening Price

The forecast opening price ($\widehat{\text{Open}}_{t+1}$) is calculated from baseline close ($C_t$) and predicted gap return ($\hat{g}$):

$\widehat{\text{Open}}_{t+1} = C_t \times \left(1 + \frac{\hat{g}}{100}\right)$
Step C: Event-Adjusted Implied Volatility Channel ($M_t$)

The daily implied volatility ($\sigma_{daily}$) is multiplied by the active Macro Event Risk Multiplier ($M_t \in [1.0, 1.5]$):

$\sigma_{adjusted} = \left(\frac{\text{India VIX}_t}{\sqrt{252}}\right) \times M_t \quad \longrightarrow \quad \text{Upper} = \widehat{\text{Open}} \times (1 + \sigma_{adj}), \quad \text{Lower} = \widehat{\text{Open}} \times (1 - \sigma_{adj})$
