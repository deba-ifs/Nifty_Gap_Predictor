2. Prediction Formulas & Math Transparency

Mathematical transparency detailing how predicted open prices and 1-sigma bounds are calculated.

Step A: Stationary Target Transformation

To prevent machine learning trees from stalling at historical ceilings, the model forecasts the stationary gap percentage ($\hat{g}$):

$\hat{g} = \widehat{\Delta \text{Gap}\%}_{t+1} = f\Big(\text{CLV}_t, \text{VIX}_t, \text{GIFT}_t, \text{PCR}_t, \text{RSI}_{14,t}, \text{SPX}_t\Big)$
Step B: Reconstructing Predicted Opening Price

The forecast opening price ($\widehat{\text{Open}}_{t+1}$) is calculated from baseline close ($C_t$) and predicted gap return ($\hat{g}$):

$\widehat{\text{Open}}_{t+1} = C_t \times \left(1 + \frac{\hat{g}}{100}\right)$
Step C: Implied 1-Sigma Volatility Bounds (Rule of 16)

Annualized India VIX ($\sigma_{ann}$) is converted to daily standard deviation assuming 252 trading days ($\sqrt{252} \approx 15.937$):

$\sigma_{daily} = \frac{\text{India VIX}_t}{\sqrt{252}} \quad \longrightarrow \quad \text{Upper} = \widehat{\text{Open}} \times (1 + \sigma_{daily}), \quad \text{Lower} = \widehat{\text{Open}} \times (1 - \sigma_{daily})$
